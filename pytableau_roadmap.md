# pytableau roadmap

The current, comprehensive roadmap is maintained in [docs/roadmap.md](docs/roadmap.md),
with a [machine-readable plan](docs/roadmap.json) and linked GitHub issues/milestones.
It is grounded in the released 3.0.0 baseline and separates shipped, partial, planned
and research work. The old milestone checklist is superseded; its history remains in Git.

Release targets are proposed sequencing, not delivery-date commitments. See the roadmap
for priorities, dependencies, acceptance criteria, release gates and current scope limits.
